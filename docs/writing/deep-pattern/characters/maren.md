## Want (protagonist_desire)
Prove Callum's death wasn't an accident. Surface want: justice. Real want: stop feeling responsible — she was supposed to be on that dive and called in sick.

## Misbelief (protagonist_wound)
Believes she's a coward. Called in sick because she had a bad feeling about the dive and didn't warn Callum. Thinks real courage means never flinching. But her "bad feeling" was correct pattern recognition from 30 years of diving — she sensed something wrong with the structure.

## Arc (character_arc_type)
Stops believing courage is about not being afraid. Understands that her instinct to flinch was the same pattern-recognition that lets her see what nobody else can. The coward becomes the witness.

## Antagonist Function
Leviathan Subsea corporation, specifically Regional Director Pryce — signed off on Callum's dive, spent 3 years burying the incident report. Can't step aside because the pattern was designed, built under contract for someone they can't name.
